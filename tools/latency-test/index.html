1 · Loopback

The device plays short chirps and listens for them with its own microphone. That gives the real speaker → mic round trip, not just what the browser reports.

Use the speaker you'll actually play with, volume at about 70%, in a fairly quiet room. Keep the phone flat with nothing covering the speaker or mic. Allow the microphone when asked.

Compare audio settings:

Round trip = speaker delay + microphone delay, measured. Output ≈ round trip minus the mic delay the browser reports (when it reports one). Reported is what Chrome claims for output (baseLatency + outputLatency) — the app uses it to line up sounds with scoring. If measured output is much bigger than reported, timing calibration absorbs the difference for the games.
